Grading tool for the AI grading activity

This tool works like Gradescope: you see one answer at a time, click the rubric items that apply, and add a point adjustment if needed. It keeps a rough record of the time you spend on each answer, and at the end it opens Form 1 with your scores filled in.

Start by opening the activity packet PDF you downloaded from the Google Drive folder. The file stays on your computer: it is read by your browser and never uploaded.

Your progress is saved in this browser, so if you close the tab you can reopen the packet and continue. Instructions: activity page.
